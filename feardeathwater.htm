    IN SUMMARY - Stacey Smith?
     Wow is there a lot of fanwank in here! Not just the masses of continuity - and you can always tell fanwank by the millions of TARDIS materialisations - but the sheer amateurishness of having the author Mary-Sue herself into the narrative is shocking. This is a mashup of Horror of Fang Rock (said rock is even pictured in the front illustration) and the Telos novella Rip Tide, only it's a lot weaker than either of those. If you can put all those things aside, then it's an okay adventure tale with a rushed climax that would have been a lot stronger if it had focused on the adventure at hand. Grace is an excellent character, although it's a pity that the most poignant thing about her is something we're told in the Author's Note. It's good that the books are trying to diversify their authorship, but this one really needed some hand-holding to avoid the excesses, and that appears to have been absent.
