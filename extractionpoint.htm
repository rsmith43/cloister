    IN SUMMARY - Stacey Smith?
     For what it is, this is a high-octain rollicking adventure that keeps the tension up. It's a shame the ninth Doctor doesn't appear for so long, and then only in cameos, but the main character is likeable enough. Howeverm, the second Doctor is appalling, one of the worst depictions ever (and that's saying something), so it's a good thing he's barely in it. Not a bad pageturner otherwise, immersed in some of the loopier corners of Doctor Who lore. But from a wider perspective, for those of us who picked this up... Where's the story so far? Where's the list of previous and post-Doom stories if we want to find out more? These multi-media tales really need a strong throughline and a reason to find the other pieces, or even just a bit of a summary of what we're dealing with. It's a crying shame there's nothing like this here.
